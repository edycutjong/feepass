FeePass lets a wallet that holds PUSD but no spendable BCH send PUSD on Bitcoin Cash mainnet: a covenant pool lane pays the miner fee, and the sender pays exactly 0.01 PUSD back in the same transaction.

Reading the pool from mainnet…

Try it: about 60 seconds

  1. Open feepass.edycu.dev on a laptop and press Connect wallet. Scan the QR with a WizardConnect wallet (for example Cashonize or Selene) and approve. The app is mainnet only.
  2. No PUSD? Press Get 0.25 starter PUSD: one claim per address, 3 per UTC day, from the faucet shown live above. No BCH is needed for it.
  3. Send 0.10 PUSD. The recipient is prefilled (it returns to the faucet). Press Send and approve one signature in your wallet: it signs only your own coin.
  4. Read the ticket: what you sent, the 0.01 PUSD you paid, the lane that paid the miner fee, your spendable BCH before and after (read from the chain), and an explorer link.

Already hold PUSD? Skip step 2. Want to see it without a wallet? The receipts below are real mainnet transactions.

Reproduce

# the app, reading Bitcoin Cash mainnet
git clone https://github.com/edycutjong/feepass && cd feepass && npm ci && npm run dev
# the covenant, builder and flow, offline on the BCH VM (no keys)
npm test
# operator: one real sponsored send on mainnet through a funded lane (needs a funded hot-wallet key and a written cap)
npx tsx scripts/mainnet-send.ts --network mainnet --authorized-cap <bch>

Honest limits

  1. One funded lane today (target 20). Two sends in the same moment: the second is asked to re-sign on another lane or retry.
  2. No named wallet verified yet. The WizardConnect flow is protocol-tested through the real relay with a headless wallet; named-wallet results go on /gate.html.
  3. The starter faucet is small: 0.25 PUSD per address, 3 claims per UTC day. If it says "out for now", use PUSD you already hold.
  4. The covenant does not pick the recipient. Your own signature (sighash 0x61) binds the outputs, as far as your wallet shows them.
  5. Not "zero BCH". A token coin always carries a few hundred sats of dust; the claim is no spendable BCH.
